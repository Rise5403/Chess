Getting started

  1. White always moves first.
  2. Click one of your pieces to select it.
  3. Dots mark quiet moves, rings mark captures.
  4. Only legal moves are offered, so you cannot leave your own king in check.
  5. A pawn reaching the far rank opens a menu to choose its new piece.

What each piece is worth

A rough guide for deciding whether a trade is good. The king has no value here because it is never captured.

Pawn — 1
Moves forward, captures diagonally.
Knight and bishop — 3
The knight jumps; the bishop never leaves its colour.
Rook — 5
Ranks and files, any distance.
Queen — 9
Rook and bishop combined.

Ways a game ends

  • Checkmate — the king is attacked and nothing stops it.
  • Stalemate — no legal move, but no check. A draw.
  • Threefold repetition — the same position three times.
  • Fifty-move rule — fifty moves each with no capture and no pawn move.
  • Insufficient material — neither side has enough left to mate.
